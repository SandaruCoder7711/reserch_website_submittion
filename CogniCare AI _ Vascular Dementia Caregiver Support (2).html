CogniCare: Personalized Multimodal AI for Vascular Dementia Caregiver Support & Tele-Rehabilitation

Bridging the gap between episodic clinical reviews and continuous home telemetry using real-time neuroimaging analysis, affective stress tracking, smart nutrition, and markerless pose estimation.

Core Subsystems

Four components, one caregiver platform

Each subsystem addresses a different stage of home care for vascular dementia.

1

Multimodal Cognitive Assessment & Atrophy Alerting

Combines 2D structural T1-weighted MRI morphometrics (EfficientNet-B0), acoustic speech DSP, and dynamic randomized MMSE into a unified Brain Health Index (BHI).

Key innovation: Exponentially Weighted Moving Average (EWMA) statistical process control for automatic atrophy alerting.
2

Intelligent Stress Management & Support

Continuous facial expression tracking using PyTorch EmotionCNN mapped into operational stress levels.

Key innovation: Random Forest model predicting near-term (30-minute) behavioral distress with real-time de-escalation guidance.
3

Smart Nutrition Routine Engine

Multi-target CatBoost classification evaluating 11 health features to auto-generate personalized meal plans, texture modifications, and patterns.

Key innovation: Deterministic clinical safety gates automatically routing renal and dysphagia risks to specialist queues.
4

Edge Biomechanical Telerehabilitation

On-device 33-landmark pose estimation via MediaPipe BlazePose (WASM) to monitor physical therapy without hardware sensors.

Key innovation: Finite-state machine fall detection with client-side Web Audio 880 Hz emergency siren.
The Methodology

Scientific Foundation

A multimodal AI pipeline built to replace episodic clinical review with continuous home monitoring.

Research Problem

Vascular dementia progresses in a stepwise, unpredictable trajectory. Episodic hospital visits fail to capture sub-acute atrophy, home rehabilitation is prone to fall hazards, and generic nutrition tools ignore dysphagia risks.

Research Gap

Episodic clinical reviews and generic tools leave caregivers without continuous, personalized telemetry at home.

Approach

Neuroimaging, speech, facial affect, nutrition and pose estimation combined in one web platform for caregivers.
